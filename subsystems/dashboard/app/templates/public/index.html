{{ ic("lifebuoy") }} FAQ

Questions, answered

What is RustDesk, and what do my technicians need? {{ ic("plus") }}

RustDesk is a fast, open-source remote desktop client. Your technicians use the free RustDesk app, pointed at your relay (your dashboard shows the settings); your customers run the branded installer you send them.

How does the free trial work? {{ ic("plus") }}

You get {{ trial_days }} days with everything included. A card is required to start, and we email you three days before the trial ends. Cancel before then and you're not charged; otherwise your plan starts automatically.

How am I billed? {{ ic("plus") }}

Through Stripe, on your plan's billing period. Your dashboard shows your plan, current usage and next invoice, and you can change your plan, card or billing details anytime.

Can I use my own domain? {{ ic("plus") }}

Yes. Add a domain like rds.yourcompany.com, create one DNS record, and we issue its certificate. Your original address keeps working too, so nothing breaks if you change domains later.

What happens to my data if I cancel? {{ ic("plus") }}

Remote support stops, and for 30 days you can still sign in to export your data, or resubscribe and pick up exactly where you left off, with the same devices and settings. After that, it's deleted.

Can I run it on my own server instead? {{ ic("plus") }}

Yes. RustDesk Fleet is open source, and the same software can be installed on your own Ubuntu server. See the project on GitHub.
